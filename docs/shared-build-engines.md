# Shared build interactions

This is the implementation map for the electrical-build update. It covers the 21 shared Arcana and nine shared boss rewards in `PowerRegistry`. It describes current behavior and explicit limits; it is not evidence that an individual power needs a balance change.

## Shared rules in this update

- **Hit** means accepted enemy damage from a registered effect. Melee, Razor Wind, Blast, returning blades, Orbit cuts, damaging dash effects, fields and shades can contribute to Storm Crown. Pure Slow, marks, movement and displacement do not count as damage.
- **Dash** describes effects produced through a dash. It does not make all movement emit dash effects: Static Wake still draws only during the normal dash, not during walking, Blast recoil or Orbit.
- **Electric** describes Static Wake and Storm Crown damage. Crown's input is **Hit**, not Electric-only. No named Wake/Crown pair is required, and the Electric label itself grants no additional trigger.
- **Slow** is the enemy's actual reduced-movement state. Crown level 2+ can gain one additional jump from an already Slowed original target or an accepted jump onto an already Slowed enemy. Any qualifying Slow source, including a teammate, can supply it. Slow applied by the current hit is not retroactively present before that hit.
- **Mark**, **Field** and **Echo** describe existing mechanics; they are not new universal condition or trigger registries. Wraithstep and Eclipse Mark retain separate owner-specific records. Field geometry and cadence remain specific to each power. Double retains its deliberate-attack copy rules.

Each original action has one Crown allowance. Every damaged enemy contributes at most once for that action, even when melee, wind, a delayed blade, a field and an echo reach it. A new enemy can advance the counter until that action discharges; after discharge, that action cannot charge or discharge Crown again. Wake, Phantom Step and the Orbit following an accepted dash share that dash's action; deliberate attacks made during Orbit are separate actions. A chain never revisits a target. Crown-generated damage and its descendants cannot generate another Crown discharge. This is separate from existing primary/secondary damage, kill-credit and launch-suppression rules.

Static Wake stores at most two ribbons per player. Overlap is a union: an enemy receives one damage contribution per owner per shared 0.25-second tick, with fractional damage carried forward. The rate is the mapped Wake damage multiplied by six per second. Lifetime starts with the first traveled segment; adding segments or ending a dash does not refresh it. A third ribbon replaces the oldest. There is no completion explosion. Level 2 changes mapped damage/lifetime/radius; level 3 adds Slow. Walking, recoil, Orbit, pauses and position resets cannot fabricate dash distance.

Reward percentages retain the game's coefficient convention: Wake's 45% level-1 coefficient becomes **270% of Damage per second**, and its 105% Prismatic coefficient becomes **630% per second**. Runtime first maps the integer Wake damage, then accumulates the continuous rate; integer rounding and enemy defenses affect observed health loss. Descriptions obtain the six-per-second multiplier from the controller.

## Arcana map

Roles below mean **generator** (produces a contact, condition or usable position), **modifier** (changes an existing attack or movement), and **payoff** (uses a prepared state). A power can serve more than one role.

| Arcana | Trigger and output | Role | Current connections and limits |
|---|---|---|---|
| Razor Wind | A deliberate swing adds an outer attack band beyond normal melee reach. | Generator / modifier | Its Hit contacts can charge Crown; attack geometry can be echoed. Inner melee and outer wind retain distinct geometry and share their original action. |
| Execution Edge | Attack cadence empowers a deliberate strike; charged Blast participates in the existing attack cadence. | Modifier / payoff | Strengthens applicable strike damage and the resulting shade copy. Automatic field ticks and echoes do not become new deliberate attacks. |
| Rupture Wave | Strike contacts release nearby shockwaves; level 2 adds Slow and level 3 a bounded secondary chain. | Generator / payoff | Wave Hits can charge Crown; Slow prepares its extra jump and Hunter's Snare. Rupture's own hit/chain bounds remain in force. |
| Aegis Field | Timed guard pulses grant resistance and Slow nearby enemies. | Generator / modifier | Supplies Slow for Crown and Snare. The guard pulse itself deals no damage and therefore contributes no Hit contact. |
| Hunter's Snare | Applicable strike contacts apply Slow; striking already Slowed targets gains bonus damage. Higher levels extend support to existing area-damage paths and Slow duration. | Generator / payoff | Any qualifying Slow can enable the existing bonus. Snare's modifier is not automatically applied to every newly registered Hit effect. Newly applied Slow cannot retroactively grant its bonus or Crown conduction to that hit. |
| Phantom Step | Normal dash contacts damage and Slow enemies. | Generator | Provides Hit/Dash contacts and Slow, works without attacking, and records movement contact for shade placement. Repeated contact retains its existing per-dash limits. |
| Riftpunch | Ending a dash primes the next eligible melee finisher, with contact grace; later levels add Slow and a shockwave. | Modifier / payoff | Direct bonus and shockwave Hits interact with Crown; Slow prepares other payoffs. The finisher's source/window rules remain specific to Riftpunch. |
| Reaper Step | Credited kills refresh dash; later levels add its existing chain/grace benefits. | Modifier / payoff | Damage from blades, fields and other credited effects can supply kills, yielding another opportunity for Wake, Phantom Step and shade placement. It does not emit a Hit merely by refreshing dash. |
| Static Wake | Normal dash draws an Electric damaging ribbon; level 3 also Slows. | Generator | Works in dash-only builds. Ribbon Hits feed Crown and its Slow can prepare later conduction. At most two ribbons, union overlap, one owner damage clock; repeated damage to one enemy within the same dash action does not repeatedly charge Crown. |
| Storm Crown | Every 3/2/1 eligible Hit contacts generates lightning to 2/3/4 additional targets at levels 1/2/3. | Payoff / generator | Accepts registered Hits from all matching effects. Level 2+ gains at most one additional jump from Slow. One discharge per original action; no target revisits or Crown ancestry recursion. Prismatic modifies its existing values once. |
| Wraithstep | Dash marks nearby enemies; subsequent qualifying strikes consume marks for bonus damage and splash/chain effects. | Generator / payoff | Existing splash/chain Hits can feed Crown; marks retain owner, expiry and remaining-strike rules. Mark is not Slow, and arbitrary field ticks do not consume marks. |
| Voidfire | Deliberate attacks manage heat; the danger band improves strike damage, while overheat detonates and briefly locks attacks. | Modifier / payoff | Detonation Hits can charge Crown; improved deliberate strike damage can carry into Double. Secondary hits do not become new heat-generating attacks. |
| Dread Resonance | Repeated qualifying melee victims build bonus damage on the currently tracked enemy; switching victim resets the focus. | Modifier / payoff | Focused damage can strengthen an applicable strike and copy. Existing credited-kill resets and multi-victim ordering remain; the visual resonance marker is not a universal Mark condition. |
| Blood Vow | Low player health multiplies applicable deliberate attack damage. | Modifier | Benefits relevant melee/Blast damage and copied strike bonuses. It does not establish a universal modifier for every field/projectile just because those effects have Hit. |
| Eclipse Mark | Eligible credited kills mark nearby enemies; subsequent qualifying strikes spend remaining mark hits for bonus damage. | Generator / payoff | Supports kill-to-next-target attack sequences. Marks are owner-specific and distinct from Wraithstep; existing Lacuna-pulse suppression remains. Mark alone does not grant Crown's Slow jump. |
| Fracture Field | Eligible kills emit randomly oriented narrow fault lines, damaging and Slowing enemies on those lines. | Generator / payoff | Fault Hits feed Crown and Slow prepares its extra jump/Snare. Fault kills cannot recursively create another Fracture Field. It is currently an instantaneous fault pattern, not a persistent floor field. |
| Farline Volley | Outer-reach melee contacts build width and flat damage; dashing resets stacks, with a level-3 payoff at full stacks. | Modifier / payoff | Later levels add Slow and a dash-triggered burst whose Hits can feed Crown. Its outer-band requirement and dash reset are intentional constraints; generic Hit sources do not automatically build Volley. |
| Sigil Chain | Qualifying strike contacts charge the next placed sigil; zones tick damage, with Slow at level 2 and a chaining damage bonus at level 3. | Generator / payoff | Field Hits and Slow connect to Crown/Snare. Hexweaver's existing Sigil Burst can detonate caught sigil zones. Zone ticks retain their original action and do not generate fresh Crown allowances. |
| Blast Drive | Hold after an accepted attack, then release a narrow forward blast and recoil backward. | Generator / modifier | Blast Hit geometry uses the deliberate-strike pipeline and can be echoed; pushes interact with Ruinous. Recoil helps reposition Crescent returns and shades, but does not draw Wake. |
| Razor Orbit | Hold through a normal dash to hook an aimed target and orbit with cutting contacts. | Generator / modifier | Cuts supply Hit/Dash contacts; manual attacks remain independent actions. Levels add column anchors and a bounded target transfer. Orbit does not draw Wake or automatically spend a shade's deliberate-attack copies. |
| Returning Crescent | A deliberate attack throws a returning blade; repositioning changes its return path. | Generator | Each enemy can take outward and return damage, but contributes only once to Crown for the originating action. Blade count, travel/cover rules and level-3 ricochet remain bounded; blades do not replay the full direct-strike proc pipeline. |

## Boss reward map

All nine boss rewards retain their existing two-pick cap.

| Reward | Trigger and output | Role | Current connections and limits |
|---|---|---|---|
| Warden's Verdict | Consecutive qualifying strike contacts build a four-contact cadence; the fourth adds a nearby burst. | Modifier / payoff | Direct bonus strengthens the strike; burst Hits may contribute to Crown. Its cadence remains in the existing strike pipeline, not every generic Hit. |
| Lacuna Echo | Eligible kills create a pulling, pulsing void zone; attacks against enemies in the zone gain the existing bonus. | Generator / payoff | Pulls can interact with Ruinous; pulse Hits can feed Crown. Pulse kills cannot renew the same Echo/Eclipse loop, preserving existing suppression. |
| Sovereign Tempo | Qualifying strike contacts build tempo; ending a dash spends it on a wave, with cooldown refund when the wave connects. | Modifier / payoff | Wave Hits have Dash and can feed Crown; refunded dash enables further movement effects. Generic Hit registration does not make every field tick build tempo. |
| Pillar Convergence | Qualifying strike cadence opens a temporary damaging pulse window around the player. | Generator / payoff | Pulses provide Hits to Crown. The window shares its originating action; cadence and lifetime remain specific to Convergence. It does not itself supply Slow. |
| Unbroken Oath | Damage resistance plus an attack bank, accelerated by multi-target deliberate strikes; a later strike spends the full bank. | Modifier / payoff | Wide attacks can fill it faster; the empowered attack's geometry and damage can be echoed. Automatic fields and echoes do not become extra bank-building primary strikes. |
| Edict of the Court | Credited kills push nearby enemies from the kill position. | Generator / payoff | Pushes can arm Ruinous and change enemy positions relative to fields. The push itself deals no damage and produces no Hit contact. |
| Null Corridor | Normal dash leaves a lasting corridor that displaces and damages enemies on its own cooldown. | Generator | Damage provides Hit/Dash; displacement can arm Ruinous. Its existing corridor geometry/cooldown remain distinct from Wake's union rule; it does not reflect Archer arrows. |
| Ruinous Impact | Direct strikes and eligible player displacement launch ordinary enemies; contact produces one burst. Immovable targets compress in place. | Payoff / generator | Burst Hits may feed Crown under their original action. Bursts cannot arm another launch; existing per-enemy launch lockout and boss/Apex immunity to displacement remain. |
| Sovereign's Double | Completed dash/recoil/orbit places one shade; subsequent deliberate melee/Blast geometry repeats at 55%, once or twice by level. | Payoff / generator | Copies applicable damage/geometry, including Razor Wind. Echo Hits share the original attack's unused Crown allowance and victim set. No repeated resource spend, movement, new shade, launch or full proc replay. |

## Connections deliberately still missing

1. **A general Mark contract.** Wraithstep, Eclipse and Dread use different records and meanings. Unifying them would require explicit duration, consumption and ownership rules; a visual icon is not enough.
2. **A general field rule.** Wake union overlap is specific to Wake. Sigils, void zones and corridors have their own lifetime and damage rules; adding the word Field does not change those rules.
3. **Universal strike modifiers.** Existing damage-breakdown effects, Warden cadence, Tempo, Convergence, Oath, Volley and sigil charge retain their current eligible strike paths. This update expands Crown's Hit input without silently turning every old power into an on-any-damage trigger.
4. **More Electric consumers.** Electric is now explicit, but it does not automatically propagate, grant Slow or enable every lightning-themed combination. Any future consumer should state its event budget and accepted property rather than name a required partner power.
5. **Unlimited echo/proc inheritance.** Double contributes Hit contacts within the original allowance; it does not run a second copy of every Arcana. This is a deliberate bound, not an unfinished promise.

These are possible future development directions, not tasks automatically added to this update. Evaluate the actual dash-only and mixed-power decisions before introducing more rules or tuning numbers.

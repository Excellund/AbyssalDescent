# Electrical-build checkpoint: historical implementation map

This records the earlier electrical-build checkpoint. For the current shared Mark, Field, generic Boon and Mission rules, use [the combat power roster](combat-power-roster.md) and [connected builds implementation](connected-builds-implementation.md). The separate Mark records, restricted Snare area list, old display names and missing-contract notes below describe that earlier state.

Static Wake's damage rate was reduced by 25% on September 11, after this checkpoint. The six-per-second rate and reward percentages recorded below are historical; see [the balance revision](static-wake-balance-20260911.md) for the current values.

This is the implementation map for the electrical-build update. It covers the 21 shared Arcana and nine shared boss rewards in `PowerRegistry`. It describes current behavior and explicit limits; it is not evidence that an individual power needs a balance change.

Player-facing descriptions follow the [combat wording guide](combat-wording.md): Attack is the deliberate control action, an attack hit is a connection, and dealing damage includes any eligible source. The implementation map below uses `Hit` for the existing internal `HIT` classification; it is not wording to copy into player descriptions.

## Shared rules in this update

- Internal **`HIT`** means accepted enemy damage from a registered effect. Melee, Razor Wind, Blast, returning blades, Orbit cuts, damaging dash effects, fields and shades can contribute to Storm Crown. Player-facing text calls this dealing damage. Pure Slow, marks, movement and displacement do not count as damage.
- **Dash** describes effects produced through a dash. It does not make all movement emit dash effects: Static Wake still draws only during the normal dash, not during walking, Blast recoil or Orbit.
- **Electric** describes Static Wake and Storm Crown damage. Crown accepts damage from any type. No named Wake/Crown pair is required, and the Electric label itself grants no additional trigger.
- **Slow** is the enemy's actual reduced-movement state. Crown level 2+ can gain one additional jump from an already Slowed original target or an accepted jump onto an already Slowed enemy. Any qualifying Slow source, including a teammate, can supply it. Slow applied by the current hit is not retroactively present before that hit.
- **Mark**, **Field** and **Echo** describe existing mechanics; they are not new universal condition or trigger registries. Wraithstep and Eclipse Mark retain separate owner-specific records. Field geometry and cadence remain specific to each power. Double retains its deliberate-attack copy rules.

Each original action has one Crown allowance. Every damaged enemy contributes at most once for that action, even when melee, wind, a delayed blade, a field and an echo reach it. A new enemy can advance the counter until that action discharges; after discharge, that action cannot charge or discharge Crown again. Wake, Phantom Step and the Orbit following an accepted dash share that dash's action; deliberate attacks made during Orbit are separate actions. A chain never revisits a target. Crown-generated damage and its descendants cannot generate another Crown discharge. This is separate from existing primary/secondary damage, kill-credit and launch-suppression rules.

Static Wake stores at most two ribbons per player. Overlap is a union: an enemy receives one damage contribution per owner per shared 0.25-second tick, with fractional damage carried forward. The rate is the mapped Wake damage multiplied by six per second. Lifetime starts with the first traveled segment; adding segments or ending a dash does not refresh it. A third ribbon replaces the oldest. There is no completion explosion. Level 2 changes mapped damage/lifetime/radius; level 3 adds Slow. Walking, recoil, Orbit, pauses and position resets cannot fabricate dash distance.

Reward percentages retain the game's coefficient convention: Wake's 45% level-1 coefficient becomes **270% of Damage per second**, and its 105% Prismatic coefficient becomes **630% per second**. Runtime first maps the integer Wake damage, then accumulates the continuous rate; integer rounding and enemy defenses affect observed health loss. Descriptions obtain the six-per-second multiplier from the controller.

## Connections deliberately still missing

1. **A general Mark contract.** Wraithstep, Eclipse and Dread use different records and meanings. Unifying them would require explicit duration, consumption and ownership rules; a visual icon is not enough.
2. **A general field rule.** Wake union overlap is specific to Wake. Sigils, void zones and corridors have their own lifetime and damage rules; adding the word Field does not change those rules.
3. **Universal strike modifiers.** Existing damage-breakdown effects, Warden cadence, Tempo, Convergence, Oath, Volley and sigil charge retain their current eligible strike paths. This update expands Crown's Hit input without silently turning every old power into an on-any-damage trigger.
4. **More Electric consumers.** Electric is now explicit, but it does not automatically propagate, grant Slow or enable every lightning-themed combination. Any future consumer should state its event budget and accepted property rather than name a required partner power.
5. **Unlimited echo/proc inheritance.** Double contributes Hit contacts within the original allowance; it does not run a second copy of every Arcana. This is a deliberate bound, not an unfinished promise.

These are possible future development directions, not tasks automatically added to this update. Evaluate the actual dash-only and mixed-power decisions before introducing more rules or tuning numbers.

## Distinguish Attack from dealing damage

The user's established reading of Hit was the left-mouse attack. Player-facing shared rules therefore use **Attack** for the deliberate input action, **attack hit** when that attack connects, and **dealing damage** for any damage source. Electric describes a damage type, independently of whether an Attack, dash effect or field produced it. Avoid capitalized Hit as an umbrella player keyword, and use `when you deal Electric damage` for future Electric-specific receivers. Internal `HIT` identifiers and serialized contexts retain their existing meanings and format; this is a presentation change.

Crown's text now says dealing damage builds its charge and retains all per-action/target and ancestry limits. The legacy descriptions distinguish attack connections from general damage, while Execution Edge continues to count attacks even when they miss. Eclipse Mark and Fracture Field describe credited kills generally. Existing implementation exceptions remain explicit: Farline Volley's level-3 dash burst enters the attack-hit resolver and can trigger its combo/mark/shockwave rules, while ordinary Wake, Orbit, Crescent, Crown and Double damage does not. Riftpunch accepts melee and charged Blast but excludes Razor Wind-only contacts. This wording pass does not alter those interactions or introduce a universal charge resource.

# New features to try

The normal desktop playtest includes these additions from `codex/uncharted-descent-20260912`. The [Practice encounter worklog](practice-encounters-worklog-20260913.md) records the latest encounter-selection changes and verification. The [sandbox worklog](practice-sandbox-worklog-20260912.md) and [workday journal](uncharted-descent-20260912.md) preserve earlier delivery records.

## Start from the menu

- **Practice** opens a configurable solo sandbox. Choose any Vessel, a full build, a named encounter, floor, Bearing and biome before Start. The encounter generates its real enemies, layout and objective from those settings. Boon stacks, Arcana/boss levels, supported Prismatic choices and applicable progression modifiers are editable; no unlocks are needed. Enemy AI and damage immunity are optional, and an empty arena supports movement trials. Pause to edit a draft: Resume keeps the current fight, while Apply & restart applies the complete new setup. Your normal setup, progression and saved descent remain intact. See the [Practice guide](practice-sandbox.md).
- **Glossary → Enemy Field Guide** explains fourteen ordinary and support enemies, their warning signs and possible responses. The same guide is available from Pause during a descent.
- **Glossary → Character Passives** uses exactly the same concise descriptions as Build Details. The Power Rules category has been removed; Build Keywords remain available.
- **Run History** can filter by outcome or solo/co-op. A selected run shows its build decisions in order and its damage recap when recorded. On smaller windows, scroll the menu's action list to reach the lower entries.

## During a normal descent

- **Oaths This Run**, in Pause, shows relevant goals and recorded progress. It distinguishes earned Oaths, requirements still intact, conditions met and requirements already broken. Unknown older or host-only evidence stays unknown.
- **Farshot** can appear among ordinary Boons. Each pick adds +10 to the eligible Damage basis against foes whose centers are at least 160 units from your current body when damage lands, up to three picks. Fields and Echoes use their normal damage coefficients. Your Build explains the full rule.
- **Rest Sites** offer Recover and, when available, up to two upgrades to eligible Boons you already own. Pick once; investing the visit in a Boon gives up its normal recovery. In co-op, each living player chooses their own benefit before the next doors open.
- **Relic Recovery** can appear among Missions from depth two onward. Walk over relics to pick them up, then enter the central receiver to deliver all three while fighting. Each living player carries one; death or disconnect drops it for recovery. Completion opens a Mission reward: choosing a Boon also grants Fortified for the next three encounters. It is a random route option, so it will not appear in every run.

## After a defeat

The **damage recap** shows up to six recent damage events, their sources and actual health lost. The same evidence is available when revisiting that run in History. Practice does not create History records. Older saved runs may lack the new evidence.

Reward cards, Build Details, the new learning screens and Pause Options also have clearer text and layouts at smaller window sizes. Gameplay tuning and comfort still need ordinary human playtesting; automated checks do not mark feedback accepted.

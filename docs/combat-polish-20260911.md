# Boss callout parity, Breakwater and Crescent

The player reported that original boss callouts differed from the newer bosses, Breakwater remained uninteresting, and the synthesized Crescent sound had become worse. The callout and Crescent entries were reopened through the feedback CLI; a focused Breakwater entry was appended between them in the supplied order. All three have separate claims and worktrees. Prior playtest work remains included.

Original and newer bosses already shared text rendering, but their phase coverage was inconsistent. Sovereign's Echo Dash retarget warnings checked an expired generic timer and lost their names; follow-up warning phases and health-bar placement are brought into parity without changing attack mechanics. The original/newest comparisons cover actual factory-created bosses in Main at three window sizes. See [boss callout diagnosis and verification](boss-move-callouts-20260911.md).

Breakwater's identity centers on a committed ram, a braced pause and a returning curved crest. Terrain bait breaks the sequence and rewards the player with an opening. A clear calm wake and outside water provide movement answers to the returning wave, including for a Slowed Bastion with Dash unavailable. The actual moving crest, painted boundary and swept damage agree. See [Breakwater mechanics](apex-breakwater.md) and [implementation and verification](breakwater-return-tide-20260911.md).

The player auditioned lighter and weightier versions, each including repeated throws over Riot Depth, and selected **“Lighter swish, but lower volume ever so slightly.”** The chosen lighter set is delivered 1.5 dB below the audition. The short return remains another 8 dB below the throw. Fixed variants preserve overlapping tails and avoid identical repetition or random pitch wobble. See [Crescent sound](crescent-sound-20260911.md) and [recording provenance](../sounds/crescent/README.md).

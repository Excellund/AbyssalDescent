# Card explanation copy review

Read-only mechanics audit for the card explanation / numeric upgrade-line split. These are copy proposals, not gameplay changes. Counts are visible characters before semantic styling; every draft is at most 175 characters. Select the explanation for the offered/resulting level. Keep numeric upgrades on their own line and place further rules in details. Authored keyword spans should be applied explicitly, never by broad text replacement.

## Mechanics that the presentation must preserve

- Attack means a deliberate action; attack hit means accepted contact. Blast Drive starts charging after an accepted swing, even if that swing misses. Do not imply that it requires damaging a foe. Normal taps remain immediate; 0.25-second arming and 0.65-second full charge can be stated in controls/details.
- Razor Wind is an immediate outer arc, not a traveling Projectile. Crescent is a Projectile and does not generate a new Attack. Two Crescent blades means capacity in flight, not two blades thrown by each Attack; the bounce occurs only outward. Normal walking does not replace Sovereign's Double: completing another Dash, Recoil or Orbit does.
- Wraithstep has no Burst at level 1. Later Bursts need a foe already Marked before the attack hit; one Burst per Attack, and level 3 adds damage to at most three further Marked foes. Strongest Mark is shared across players. Dread stacks are owner-specific and per foe; they survive target switches and Mark expiry, but end on owner/enemy death or room end.
- Hunter's Snare checks Slow before damage, then applies its own Slow. Level 1 boosts Attacks only; level 2+ boosts all qualifying owner damage. Level 3 doubles every owner-applied Slow duration, not only its own Attack Slow. Storm Crown's extra jump is once per chain through an already Slowed target, not once for every Slowed target; Crown descendants cannot charge it.
- Rupture Wave can damage each foe only once per originating Attack, including its level 3 continuation. Fracture is a brief set of fault-line Bursts, not a persistent Field, and its own kills cannot create another Fracture. Aegis Pulse deals no damage and leaves no Field.
- Farline stacks come from direct attack hits beyond 65% of the actual strike range; Razor Wind is excluded. Slow begins at two stacks (four Prismatic). Every Dash clears the bank; only level 3 at full stacks adds the Dash Burst. Sigil Chain arms after four accepted contacts and drops on a later connected Attack; it cannot drop on a further foe of the same arming Attack. Hexweaver's passive Burst can detonate existing sigils; that is character-specific detail, not a promise for every character.
- Reaper Step already fully refreshes Dash on any credited Kill at level 1. Level 2 can store one additional Dash from another Kill during the chain window; level 3 adds contact protection during that window. Avoid describing the structural upgrades as merely '+chain'.
- Warden counts accepted contacts, once per foe per Attack; the same foe on a later Attack counts again. Its 2.2-second timeout restarts after every contact, not after the first of four. The mapped bonus is a strength parameter used by escalating hits, not a flat amount added to every hit.
- Tempo gains one stack per connected Attack, not per enemy struck. Completing a movement spends the stacks; the Burst must damage a foe to refund Dash. Convergence cannot charge or refresh while its Field is active. Its threshold also counts connected Attacks rather than every enemy.
- Lacuna Well replaces its previous well and buffs damage inside any owner Field once; overlapping Fields cannot multiply the bonus. Its own pulse kills cannot continually regenerate wells. Null Corridor's half-second repeat limit is per trail, not a new global cooldown across all trails.
- Unbroken Oath gains from single-target attack hits as well as multi-target attacks. A full bank empowers the next deliberate Attack and is spent even on a miss; it does not guarantee that every foe in that Attack receives the bonus. Voidfire's extra damage is only at high Heat, and its Burst is only at overheat. Level 3 halves the effective lockout.
